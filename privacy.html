Privacy policy

This policy describes the current read-only Google integration of Openclaw Personal (TARS), a personal assistant operated by its owner.

Information and permissions

When the owner connects a Google account, the app requests the following permissions:

  • openid, email, and profile: basic account identity, including account identifier, email address, name, and profile information.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: read Gmail messages and related metadata, including message bodies and attachments when needed for the owner's request.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: read calendars and events, including titles, descriptions, times, locations, and participant information.

The current integration does not request Google Drive access or permission to send email or create, edit, or delete Gmail messages or Calendar events. Permissions actually granted can be reviewed in the Google consent screen and account settings.

Purpose and use

The app uses this information to answer the owner's questions, summarize email and calendar information, and support personal planning. It may retain relevant context in conversation transcripts, logs, and assistant memory.

The app does not sell personal information, use Google user data for advertising, or use Google user data to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. These statements describe the app's use, not a guarantee about every external provider's policies. The owner should review and configure provider data-use settings before enabling processing of sensitive information.

Openclaw Personal (TARS)'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Processing and disclosure

Relevant email, calendar, identity, and conversation content may be sent to the third-party AI providers configured by the owner to generate answers, and to the chosen chat service to deliver messages. Those services process information under their own terms, privacy policies, settings, and retention practices. The app does not promise that these services never retain data or use it for training.

The owner controls the local installation and records and can access them. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. This public documentation site contains no private email or calendar data and uses no analytics or external JavaScript. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Token storage and security

Google OAuth tokens are stored in an encrypted local file keyring. The owner has approved an unattended arrangement that allows the running assistant to unlock and use that keyring. Encryption at rest does not prevent access by an authorized running process or anyone who controls the owner's environment. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Transcripts, logs, cached information, and assistant memory may retain content until the owner deletes it. Local backups may also retain copies. The owner controls deletion of local records and backups; the app does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period.

To stop future Google access, remove the app's connection in Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access does not by itself delete information previously retained locally or by AI and chat providers.

For deletion, the owner should remove retained content and credentials from the local installation and applicable backups and use provider and chat-service deletion controls where available. Service retention requirements and backup schedules may continue to apply.

Contact and changes

Contact the owner through the tjmanchu GitHub profile for privacy questions. Public repository issues may be used only for non-sensitive documentation questions. Never include private email, calendar entries, account identifiers, or credentials in public posts.

This policy will be updated when the app's permissions or data handling change. The effective date above identifies this version.
