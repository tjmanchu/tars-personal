Openclaw Personal (TARS)

An owner-operated personal assistant that helps its owner find and understand information in Gmail and Google Calendar through a chosen chat interface.

What the app does

With the owner's authorization, TARS can read Gmail messages and calendar events to answer questions, summarize information, and surface upcoming commitments. Basic Google account identity information identifies the connected account.

The current Google integration is read-only. It does not request Google Drive access, send email, or create, edit, or delete messages or calendar events.

How information is handled

Requested information may be processed by configured third-party AI providers and delivered through the owner's selected chat interface. Conversation transcripts, logs, and assistant memory may retain content until the owner deletes it. Provider and chat-service retention policies may also apply.

This is a personal integration, not a public sign-up service. It is not an official Google or OpenAI product and is not endorsed by either company.
